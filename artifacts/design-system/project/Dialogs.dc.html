Game dialogs

Every modal state from the interactive prototype. Shared anatomy: a #121f2a panel with a 1px #2c4254 border and 12px radius; a header with a 24px condensed uppercase title and a 44px close button; a body with 16px gaps; actions right-aligned in the footer, primary last. Destructive actions use the red outline, never the accent fill. Narrow dialogs are 420px wide, wide ones 720px.

Rules

From the Menu. Draft summary until the gameplay doc settles the rules.

Rules

  • Roll two dice and move clockwise. Passing GO collects $200.
  • Land on an unowned property to buy it, or send it to auction.
  • Land on an owned property and pay its owner rent.
  • Doubles roll again. Three doubles in a row go to jail.
  • Leave jail by rolling doubles, paying $50 or using a card.
  • Own a full colour set to build houses, then a hotel.
  • Can't pay? Sell buildings and mortgage, or declare bankruptcy.

Draft summary; the full rules are still open in the gameplay doc.
