Component states for the lobby screens. Seats show who plays where: host, a hot-seat player on a shared device, online, or invited. Any player can add hot-seat players on their own device. The host can remove anyone; a guest can remove only the players on their own device. Removing asks first. Start game unlocks once one other player has joined.
