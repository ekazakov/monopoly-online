Game dialogs · Auction

Auction dialog states

The redesigned auction dialog in every state we need: opening, bidding, quick raise, focus, invalid input, waiting, hot-seat, pending, passed, sold, light and dark pages, and a 360 px narrow screen. The dialog is always a light paper card. Colours come from the Harborfront tokens.

Layout

The title deed is on the left, above the bidders table. Bidders in a table: Name, Last bid, Bid, Quick raise, and an action column. Only the acting seat on this device gets the bid controls. Other cells stay empty.

Current bid

The dialog has no current bid block. The bidder with the bold, bigger last bid is the leader. A screen reader gets the current bid from a hidden live region.

Row alignment

All table cells align to the vertical centre. The name and the quick buttons are centred in their cells.

Errors

An error shows in its own row directly under the acting row. It starts at the Bid column and spans the Bid and Quick raise columns.

Quick raise

Each button adds its amount to the value in the input: +$10, +$50, +$100. It does not reset to the high bid. A button that would pass the cash limit is disabled.

Focus ring

Draw the focus ring around the whole field wrapper: the $ sign and the number. Do not draw it on the inner input.

Engine rule

Any whole-dollar bid of at least the high bid + $10 is legal, and at most the bidder cash. The opening minimum is $10. The input starts at the minimum.

No timer, no withdraw

The auction has no countdown. A bidder cannot withdraw a bid. A pass is final.

Narrow screen

At 360 px the table becomes stacked rows, the deed sits above the bid and every control is at least 44 px high.

Whole numbers

The field accepts digits only. A decimal point, e, + and - are blocked, and a pasted value is cleaned to digits.

Passed bidders

A bidder who passed is removed from the table. The log keeps the pass.

Device seats

No tag marks the seats of this device. The input label names the bidder who acts.
