# Sound credits

All files here come from five Kenney packs: Casino Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, and Digital Audio. Kenney (www.kenney.nl) publishes them under Creative Commons Zero (CC0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. You can use them with no attribution, but the project lists them here.

The files are trimmed, changed in pitch, faded out, normalized to a peak of -3 dB, and converted to mono MP3 at 96 kbit/s. `coin-out.mp3` also has a low-pass filter at 1800 Hz and a peak of -6 dB. `coin-in.mp3` has two parts. The first part is the first 0.2 s of `handleCoins.ogg`. The second part is a metal ring that the project made in code. The ring has two pings at 2093 Hz and 3136 Hz, with inharmonic partials, and it lasts 1.2 s. The ring is original and has no license limit.
